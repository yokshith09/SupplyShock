# SupplyShock Orchestrator

Supply-chain disruption responder for manufacturers. Tell it about a cyclone, port strike or supplier problem and it finds which suppliers, materials and customer orders are exposed, ranks alternate suppliers, proposes scored mitigation options, and (after you approve) sends RFQs and expedite requests.

**Keywords:** supply chain, disruption, procurement, manufacturing, risk, cyclone, port strike, RFQ, supplier, exposure

**Example prompts**
- Cyclone warning on the Tamil Nadu coast, are we exposed?
- Is supplier S-07 safe right now?
- Find a backup supplier for ECU housings.
